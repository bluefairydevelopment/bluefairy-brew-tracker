BlueFairy Brew Tracker — Marketing Content
==========================================


3-SENTENCE SUMMARY
------------------

BlueFairy Brew Tracker is a free WordPress plugin that keeps a batch log for mead, wine, cider and beer — style, vessel, start date, additions, gravity readings, stage, and what is due next — with every batch stored as an ordinary record in your own database. It lives in wp-admin under its own Brew Tracker menu, works out ABV and attenuation from the readings you logged and names the formula it used, draws a gravity chart for every batch, shows what is due in a Due panel on the batch and a Next due column on the list, exports everything as CSV, and leaves your batches in place when it is uninstalled. Requires PHP 8.1+ and WordPress 6.5+ and no other plugin; licensed GPL-2.0-or-later.

CTA is doubled: the free download beside the Pro purchase. The free product URL

It must NOT carry the licence-manager meta: only the paid half issues keys.

the free plugin sends email, works out a schedule, supports Brix, or has
"unlimited" batches. The Pro card names only rows from COMPARISON.md; the
"25 recipes" count is data/recipes.php in the Pro repository (25 seed_slug
